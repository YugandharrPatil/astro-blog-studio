- we always say that the old school bodybuilders never had access to these sophisticated science based techniques and routines and blame the science based people
- the key missing part here is this: the science based stuff only makes a considerable difference in advanced/elite top of the line level lifters
  - the difference it creates is so minimal, that the regular gym goer won't even notice, but
- what matters much much much more this following these sophisticated routines is the bread and butter of lifting: sticking to the old school exercises who have survived the test of time and do: progressive overload, ~~slow~~ controlled eccentric, 2 min breaks, hydration and of course diet and sleep.
