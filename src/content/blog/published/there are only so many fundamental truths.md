title: there are only so many fundamental truths

this is my absolute most favourite quote of all time:

> “Nature uses only the longest threads to weave her patterns, so each small piece of her fabric reveals the organization of the entire tapestry.” - Richard P. Feynman

there are only so many fundamental truths that govern everything; maybe 5-10-20? depending upon how you frame the sentence.

whatever you're interested in, say wealth creation, getting fit, seducing chicks, is all governed by only so many fundamental truths. every nonsense sophisticated advice that you hear on the internet is a derivative of these fundamental truths. taken out of context; most of the time misguiding you.

then there are truths that are specific to you. that can (for gods sake) be written in simple language with simple words, there to serve you and staying forever true to yourself.

can we limit listening to advices and start depending on truths more?
